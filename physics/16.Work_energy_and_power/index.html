Energy · Intuition First

Energy Is Never Used Up

You can't create it. You can't destroy it. All you can do is move it around — from a store, through a device, into another store.

A falling ball isn't "using up" its energy. It's converting height-energy into motion-energy. A phone on charge isn't "filling up" — it's converting electrical energy into chemical bonds.

This lesson shows you every store, every conversion, and the math that connects them.

The Formulas

Numbers you can trust

Two formulas do almost all the work. Watch them run on a real 2 kg ball.

Gravitational Potential
E_p = m g h
= 2.00 × 9.8 × 3.00
E_p = 58.80 J
Kinetic Energy (at the bottom)
E_k = ½ m v²
v = √(2gh) = √(2×9.8×3) = 7.67 m/s
= ½ × 2.00 × 7.67²
E_k = 58.80 J
Work & Power
W = F s P = W / t
Lifting 2 kg through 3 m in 2 s:
W = 58.80 J · P = 29.40 W

Notice: E_p at the top = E_k at the bottom. That's conservation in one line.

How Much Is Wasted

Efficiency is the honest number

No real device converts all its input into useful output. The rest leaks away — usually as heat.

Efficiency Meter
40 %
Input
150 J
Useful
60 J
Wasted
90 J
Efficiency = 60 / 150 = 40 %

Efficiency = useful output ÷ total input × 100%

An LED is about 90% efficient. An incandescent bulb is about 5% — the other 95% is wasted heat. That's why we switched.

Where Energy Comes From

Renewable vs non-renewable

Some sources refill themselves. Others are gone once burned.

✓ Renewable

  • Solar
  • Wind
  • Hydro
  • Tidal
  • Geothermal
  • Biomass

✕ Non-renewable

  • Coal
  • Oil
  • Natural gas
  • Nuclear

The National Grid moves electrical energy from power stations to homes and industry through transmission lines. At every step — generation, transmission, conversion — some energy is lost as heat. That's why efficiency matters at grid scale.

Lock It In

What you actually learned

  • ✓9 stores, 9 conversions. Every situation in physics is a combination of these.
  • ✓Energy is conserved. GPE + KE + Thermal = constant. It never appears or disappears.
  • ✓E_p = mgh, E_k = ½mv². These two formulas carry almost every calculation.
  • ✓Efficiency = useful ÷ input. Real devices always lose some energy to heat.
