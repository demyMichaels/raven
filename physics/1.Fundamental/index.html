Every SI derived unit reduces to the 7 base units — e.g. 1 J = 1 kg·m²·s⁻², 1 V = 1 kg·m²·s⁻³·A⁻¹.

Non-SI units (bar, atm, eV, Å, Ci…) are human conventions and are excluded here.
