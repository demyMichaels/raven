Physics by Magnetic Flux

Flux = scalar count of field lines · B = vector (flux per area)

🧲
Magnetic flux Φ_B
scalar · weber (Wb)
ΦB = ∮ B→ · dA→
Scalar — a count of field lines through the surface. Unit: weber.
➡️
Magnetic flux density B
vector · tesla (T)
B→ = dΦB dA · n^
Flux per unit perpendicular area. Denser lines = larger B.
📏
Weber (Wb)
SI unit of flux
1 Wb = 1 T · m2 = 1 V · s
Named after Wilhelm Weber. Also equals a volt-second.
⚡
Tesla (T)
SI unit of B
1 T = N A·m = kg s2·A
One tesla = one newton per ampere per metre of wire.
📐
Flux at an angle θ
Φ = BA cos θ
ΦB = B A cos θ
Only the perpendicular component of B contributes: BA cos θ.
🔗
Flux linkage NΦ
weber-turns
N ΦB = NBAcosθ
Each turn links the same flux — total linkage scales with N.
🌀
Flux through a solenoid
uniform inside
B=μ0nI , ΦB=BA
Field inside is uniform; outside it is nearly zero.
🍩
Flux through a toroid
fully confined
B= μ0NI 2πr
All flux stays inside the ring — no leakage outside an ideal toroid.
🔄
Rotating coil flux
Φ(t) = BA cos ωt
ΦB (t) = BA cos (ωt)
Oscillates sinusoidally — the basis of AC generators.
⚡
Faraday's law of induction
ε = −dΦ/dt
ε = − dΦB dt
Minus sign = Lenz's law. Also ε = −N dΦ/dt for a coil.
🚫
Lenz's law
opposes change
Φinduced opposes ΔΦB
Nature resists a change in flux — the source of induction.
🚄
Motional emf from flux change
ε = BLv
ε= − dΦB dt = BLv
Area of the loop grows as the rod moves — that's the flux change.
🔒
Gauss's law for magnetism
no monopoles
∮ B→ · dA→ = 0
Every field line entering a closed surface must exit — no magnetic charge exists.
🌊
Maxwell–Faraday equation
integral form
∮ E→ · dl→ = − dΦB dt
A changing magnetic flux creates a circulating electric field.
🔁
Ampère–Maxwell law
displacement current
∮ B→ · dl→ = μ0I + μ0 ε0 dΦE dt
Changing electric flux also creates B — Maxwell's addition.
🔄
Magnetic vector potential A
B = ∇ × A
B→ = ∇ × A→
A curls around field lines. Flux through a loop = ∮A⃗·dl⃗.
🔵
Magnetic flux quantum Φ₀
h / 2e
Φ0 = h 2e ≈ 2.068×10−15 Wb
Smallest possible flux through a superconducting loop.
🔢
Fluxoid quantization
integer n
Φ = n Φ0 , n ∈ ℤ
Only integer multiples of Φ₀ are allowed — a quantum effect visible at macroscale.
🚇
Josephson junction flux
tunnelling
I= Ic sinδ , dδ dt = 2eV ℏ
Cooper pairs tunnel across — flux and voltage are linked to phase.
🎯
SQUID
flux magnetometer
V∝ cos ( πΦ Φ0 )
Can detect flux as small as 10⁻⁵ Φ₀ — used in MEG, geophysics, and quantum computing.
📌
Flux pinning
type-II superconductor
F→pin > F→Lorentz
Vortices stuck at defects let superconductors carry huge currents.
🔁
B–H hysteresis loop
ferromagnet
B= μ0 (H+M)
Loop area = energy lost per cycle. Basis of transformer core losses.
🧲
Magnetic permeability μ
B = μH
B→ = μ H→
Material property — how much a medium amplifies the applied field.
📊
Relative permeability μ_r
dimensionless
μr = μ μ0
μ_r ≈ 1 for vacuum/Cu · ≈ 10³ for iron · ≈ 10⁵ for μ-metal.
💪
Magnetomotive force (MMF)
ampere-turns
F= NI (ampere-turns)
The magnetic "pressure" driving flux around a magnetic circuit.
🚧
Reluctance
magnetic resistance
ℛ = l μA
Longer path or smaller cross-section → more reluctance → less flux.
📐
Hopkinson's law
magnetic Ohm's law
ΦB = F ℛ
Direct analogue of I = V/R for magnetic circuits.
💨
Flux leakage
Φ_use < Φ_total
Φleak = Φtotal − Φlinked
Design problem in transformers — reduces efficiency and coupling.
💥
Flux compression
B ∝ 1/A
ΦB = const ⟹ B= ΦB A ∝ 1 A
Squeezing flux into a smaller area boosts B enormously. Used in pulsed-field magnets.
☀️
Magnetic reconnection
solar flare energy
topology change ⟹ Ekinetic + radiation
Field lines break and re-join — powers solar flares, auroras, tokamak disruptions.
