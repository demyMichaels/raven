Key insight: 
Rive animates properties. 
Physics units measure properties of reality. 
The alignment is about choosing the right Rive property to represent the right physical dimension.

Let me think about each base unit:


Second (time) — 
Rive's fundamental axis is the timeline. 
Every animation IS time. 
So the second is the meta-unit of Rive itself. 
You can represent it as: a timeline scrubber, 
a pulsing clock, 
the frame counter, a pendulum. The phenomenon of "change" itself. In Rive, time is the timeline input and the animation duration. You can literally make a 1-second loop.


Metre (length/space) — In Rive, this maps to 
position (x, y), 
scale, and 
distance between objects. 
The phenomenon: spatial extension. Rive object: a ruler, a beam, a path length. You animate position/scale to represent length.



Kilogram (mass) — This is trickier. Mass isn't directly visual. But it manifests as: inertia (resistance to acceleration), weight (downward force), momentum. In Rive, you can represent mass through: the "weight" of an object (how slowly it accelerates), squash and stretch, a scale that bends, a heavy object that thuds.
 You could use Rive's physics or easing curves to show inertia.


Ampere (electric current) — Flow of charge. In Rive: particle systems, flowing lines, animated dashes along a path, a stream of dots. The phenomenon: continuous flow. Rive object: a wire with particles moving along it.


Kelvin (temperature) — Average kinetic energy. In Rive: color shifts (blue to red), vibration speed, heat wave distortion, particle jitter. The phenomenon: molecular agitation. Rive object: a thermometer, a vibrating molecule, heat shimmer.


Mole (amount of substance) — Counting entities. 
In Rive: a swarm, a cloud of particles, a counter, a grid of dots. The phenomenon: quantity/counting. Rive object: a particle system that spawns N particles.


Candela (luminous intensity) — Light emitted. In Rive: glow, opacity, radial gradients, light rays, bloom effects. The phenomenon: emission of light. Rive object: a candle, a light source with rays.
