Part 1 · The 30 Quantities

Sorted from very dilute (10⁻⁷ M) to pure water (55.5 M).

🧪
Molarity (c or M)
mol/L
c=nV
n / V → mol m³ → mol · m⁻³
Moles of solute per litre of solution — the standard unit.
⚖️
Molality (b or m)
mol/kg solvent
b=nsolutemsolvent
mol / kg → mol kg → mol · kg⁻¹
Temperature-independent — used in precise physical chemistry.
📊
Mole fraction x_i
dimensionless
xi= nintot
mol / mol → units cancel → dimensionless (0 → 1)
Mole fraction of one component among all molecules.
🏋️
Mass concentration ρ_i
g/L
ρi= miV
m / V → kg m³ → kg · m⁻³
Used when molecular weight is unknown or irrelevant.
⚗️
Mass fraction w_i
dimensionless
wi= mimtot
kg / kg → units cancel → dimensionless (0 → 1)
Ratio by mass — the basis of the "percent by weight" label.
🔬
Parts per million (ppm)
10⁻⁶ ratio
ppm= msolutemtotal ×106
mg/kg → 10⁻⁶ · dimensionless
Trace pollutants, mineral content, atmospheric gases.
🧫
Parts per billion (ppb)
10⁻⁹ ratio
ppb= msolutemtotal ×109
µg/kg → 10⁻⁹ · dimensionless
Trace contaminants, doping, isotopic abundance.
🧬
Osmolarity
osmol/L
osmolarity=i·c
i · mol/L → osmol · m⁻³
i = number of particles per formula unit. NaCl has i = 2.

eq/L

Legacy unit. 1 M H₂SO₄ = 2 N because it donates two protons.
