Part 1 · The 30 Quantities

Each card reduces to m·s⁻¹ — unless noted.

🏁
Speed
scalar · m/s
v= distancetime
distance / time → m s → m · s⁻¹
Magnitude only — direction not specified.
➡️
Velocity
vector · m/s
v→= Δx→ Δt
Δx⃗ / Δt → m s → m · s⁻¹ + direction
Direction matters — this is the whole point.
📊
Average speed
scalar
vavg= total pathtotal time
total distance / total time → m · s⁻¹
Distance, not displacement.
⚡
Instantaneous velocity
limit Δt → 0
v→= limΔt→0 Δx→ Δt = dx→ dt
lim Δx⃗ / Δt → the limit preserves units → m · s⁻¹
Tangent to the path at that instant.
➡️
Constant velocity
a⃗ = 0
v→=constant , a→=0
[v⃗] unchanged · [a⃗] = m · s⁻² = m · s⁻¹
Same speed, same direction, forever.
🪂
Terminal velocity
steady state
vt= 2mg ρACd
√(kg · m · s⁻² / (kg·m⁻³ · m²)) → √(m² · s⁻²) → m · s⁻¹
Weight balanced by drag — no more acceleration.
🌍
Escape velocity
11.2 km/s
vesc= 2GMr
√(G · M / r) → √(m³·kg⁻¹·s⁻² · kg / m) → m · s⁻¹
Minimum speed to leave a planet's gravity well.
🛰️
Orbital velocity
tangential
vorb= GMr
√(G · M / r) → √(m³·kg⁻¹·s⁻² · kg / m) → m · s⁻¹
Velocity is always tangent to the orbit.
🔄
Angular velocity
rad/s
ω= ΔθΔt
Δθ / Δt → angle (rad) is dimensionless → rad · s⁻¹ ≡ s⁻¹
Same ω, all points on a rigid body.
📏
Linear velocity (v = ωr)
tangential
v=ωr
ω · r → rad · s⁻¹ · m → m · s⁻¹
Velocity points tangent to the circle, not along the spoke.
🔀
Relative velocity
vector subtraction
v→AB = v→A − v→B
m·s⁻¹ − m·s⁻¹ → subtraction of like units → m · s⁻¹
Velocity of A as seen from B's frame.
🔌
Drift velocity
electrons
vd= InAq
A / (m⁻³ · m² · C) = A · s / C = m · s⁻¹
Electrons drift opposite to conventional current I.
📈
RMS speed
gas molecules
vrms= 3kTm
√(k·T / m) = √(kg·m²·s⁻² / kg) = m · s⁻¹
Root of the mean of the squares — always ≥ average speed.
🎯
Most probable speed
peak of distribution
vp= 2kTm
√(2·k·T / m) = √(kg·m²·s⁻² / kg) = m · s⁻¹
Peak of the Maxwell–Boltzmann curve.
🌊
Group velocity
envelope
vg= dωdk
dω / dk = (rad·s⁻¹) / (rad·m⁻¹) = m · s⁻¹
Speed of the envelope — where the energy travels.
〰️
Phase velocity
crest speed
vp= ωk
ω / k = (rad·s⁻¹) / (rad·m⁻¹) = m · s⁻¹
Speed of a single crest (or trough) along the wave.
💨
Wind velocity
vector
v→=speed+direction
speed + bearing → m · s⁻¹ + direction
Weather reports give speed and direction.
🔊
Speed of sound
343 m/s in air
v=γRTM
√(γ·R·T / M) = √(J·kg⁻¹) = m · s⁻¹
Direction of propagation is well-defined — usually treated as velocity.
💡
Speed of light
3 × 10⁸ m/s
c≈3.00×108 m/s
defined as 299 792 458 m / s → m · s⁻¹ + exact by definition
Universal speed limit in vacuum.
✈️
Mach number
dimensionless ratio
Ma= vcs
m·s⁻¹ / m·s⁻¹ = units cancel → dimensionless (Ma = 1)
Ma is a ratio, not a speed. Ma = 1 is the sound barrier.
🛬
Ground speed
w.r.t. ground
v→ground= v→air+ v→wind
m·s⁻¹ + m·s⁻¹ = vector sum → m · s⁻¹
Vector sum of airspeed and wind velocity.
🛩️
Airspeed
w.r.t. air
v→air= v→ground− v→wind
m·s⁻¹ − m·s⁻¹ = like units subtract → m · s⁻¹
Speed relative to the air the plane flies through.
🌌
Recession velocity
v = H₀ d
v=H0d
H₀ · d = (km·s⁻¹·Mpc⁻¹) · Mpc = km · s⁻¹
Hubble–Lemaître law: farther galaxies recede faster.
⏱️
Proper velocity
dx/dτ
w→= dx→dτ = γv→
dx⃗ / dτ = γ (dimensionless) · m·s⁻¹ = m · s⁻¹
Uses proper time τ — can exceed c without violating relativity.
4️⃣
Four-velocity
spacetime vector
Uμ= γ(c,v→)
U⁰ = m · s⁻¹ · U¹ U² U³ same units
Time component is γc; space component is γv⃗. All four share m·s⁻¹.
🚫
Superluminal motion
not physical
v>c → not realized in nature
[v] = m·s⁻¹ but forbidden by relativity
Listed only to correct the misconception.
🐢
Slow speed
v ≪ c
v≪c
[v] = m·s⁻¹ with γ → 1 (Newtonian limit)
Newtonian mechanics applies — no relativistic corrections needed.
⚡
Relativistic velocity
γ → ∞ as v → c
γ= 1 1− v2c2
[γ] = dimensionless · [v] = m·s⁻¹ unchanged
Time dilation and length contraction grow without bound near c.
⚛️
Fermi velocity
electron gas
vF= ℏkFm
ℏ·k_F / m = (J·s) · m⁻¹ / kg = m · s⁻¹
Electrons at the Fermi surface move fast even at absolute zero.
🌀
Planck speed
c
vP= GℏGℏ c = c
√(ℏ·G / c³) · c⁴ / (ℏ·G) = Planck length / Planck time = m · s⁻¹ = c
The only speed built from fundamental constants — equals c.
