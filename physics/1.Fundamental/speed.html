Physics by Speed & Velocity

Speed = round lane (scalar) · Velocity = arrow (vector)

🏁
Speed
scalar · m/s
v= distance time
Magnitude only — direction not specified.
➡️
Velocity
vector · m/s
v → = Δx→ Δt
Direction matters — this is the whole point.
📊
Average speed
scalar
vavg = total path length total time
Distance, not displacement.
⚡
Instantaneous velocity
limit Δt → 0
v→ = lim Δt→0 Δx→ Δt = dx→ dt
Tangent to the path at that instant.
➡️
Constant velocity
a⃗ = 0
v→ = constant , a→ = 0
Same speed, same direction, forever.
🪂
Terminal velocity
steady state
vt = 2mg ρACd
Weight balanced by drag — no more acceleration.
🌍
Escape velocity
11.2 km/s
vesc = 2GM r ≈ 11.2 km/s
Minimum speed to leave a planet's gravity well.
🛰️
Orbital velocity
tangential
vorb = GM r
Velocity is always tangent to the orbit.
🔄
Angular velocity
rad/s
ω= Δθ Δt
Rate of change of angle — same ω, all points on a rigid body.
📏
Linear velocity (v = ωr)
tangential
v=ωr
Velocity points tangent to the circle, not along the spoke.
🔀
Relative velocity
vector subtraction
v→ AB = v→ A − v→ B
Velocity of A as seen from B's frame.
🔌
Drift velocity
electrons
vd = I nAq
Electrons drift opposite to conventional current I.
📈
RMS speed
gas molecules
vrms = 3kT m
Root of the mean of the squares — always ≥ average speed.
🎯
Most probable speed
peak of distribution
vp = 2kT m
Peak of the Maxwell–Boltzmann curve.
🌊
Group velocity
envelope
vg = dω dk
Speed of the envelope — where the energy travels.
〰️
Phase velocity
crest speed
vp = ω k
Speed of a single crest (or trough) along the wave.
💨
Wind velocity
vector
v→ = speed + direction
Weather reports give speed and direction.
🔊
Speed of sound
343 m/s in air
v=γRTM
Direction of propagation is well-defined — usually treated as velocity.
💡
Speed of light
3 × 10⁸ m/s
c≈3.00×108 m/s
Universal speed limit in vacuum.
✈️
Mach number
dimensionless ratio
Ma= v cs
Ma is a ratio, not a speed. Ma = 1 is the sound barrier.
🛬
Ground speed
w.r.t. ground
v→ ground = v→ air + v→ wind
Vector sum of airspeed and wind velocity.
🛩️
Airspeed
w.r.t. air
v→ air = v→ ground − v→ wind
Speed relative to the air the plane flies through.
🌌
Recession velocity
v = H₀ d
v= H0 d
Hubble–Lemaître law: farther galaxies recede faster.
⏱️
Proper velocity
dx/dτ
w→ = dx→ dτ = γv→
Uses proper time τ — can exceed c without violating relativity.
4️⃣
Four-velocity
spacetime vector
Uμ = γ(c,v→)
Time component is γc; space component is γv⃗. Always null-normed.
🚫
Superluminal motion
not physical
v>c → not realized in nature
Listed only to correct the misconception.
🐢
Slow speed
v ≪ c
v≪c
Newtonian mechanics applies — no relativistic corrections needed.
⚡
Relativistic velocity
γ → ∞ as v → c
γ= 1 1− v2 c2
Time dilation and length contraction grow without bound near c.
⚛️
Fermi velocity
electron gas
vF = ℏkF m
Electrons at the Fermi surface move fast even at absolute zero.
🌀
Planck speed
c
vP = c 1 = c ≈ 3.00×108 m/s
The only speed built from fundamental constants — equals c.
