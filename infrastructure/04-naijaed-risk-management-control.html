Definitions, categories, appetite and the inherent-vs-residual lens we apply to every naira spent.

35.2 RISK & RISK MANAGEMENT

🎲 What risk means here

Risk is the effect of uncertainty on our cost base and runway objectives. Risk management is the coordinated activity of identifying, assessing, controlling and monitoring those uncertainties so the ₦150M runway is not eroded by foreseeable events.

35.3 CATEGORIES OF RISK

🗂️ Six categories in our register

  • Financial — FX, fraud, wage inflation
  • Operational — gateway concentration, support creep
  • Compliance — NDPC / data protection
  • Technology — cyber breach, hosting outage
  • Strategic — key person, CAC, revenue concentration
  • Legal — content / IP infringement
35.4 CONCEPTS

📐 Appetite, likelihood, impact

Risk appetite: zero tolerance for child-data breach or regulatory non-compliance; low appetite for FX exposure above 15% of cost base; moderate appetite for sub-4-hour outages; accept market-driven wage inflation.

Score = Likelihood × Impact (1–5 each, max 25). Inherent = before controls. Residual = after controls.

35.4 TOLERANCE

📏 What we will and won't accept

Every risk must land in the Low band (≤6) after controls, or it escalates to the founders in the monthly risk review. Risks that cannot be reduced below Low must be avoided — we do not accept them at any price.

35.7

Embedding risk into the operating rhythm

Risk only works when it lives inside daily decisions, not in a document.

🗣️ Tone at the top

Risk is the first agenda item in the monthly founder review. No growth metric is celebrated if it breaches a risk tolerance.

🎯 Risk in OKRs

Every team OKR carries one linked KRI. Engineering owns patch SLA, growth owns CAC trend, support owns tickets/user.

💸 Spend gate

Any new vendor or tool above ₦100k/month requires a mini risk assessment: FX exposure, vendor concentration, exit cost.

🧑‍🏫 Training & documentation

Quarterly NDPC / data-handling training for all staff. The risk register is a living document reviewed monthly, not annual.

35.8

Methods of controlling risk — TARA

Every control in the register maps to one of four responses.

🔄 Transfer

Shift the financial consequence to a third party.

  • Cyber liability insurance (R5)
  • Key-person insurance (R7)
  • FX forward contract (R1)
  • Contractual IP indemnities (R8)

🚫 Avoid

Eliminate the activity that creates the risk.

  • Never store raw card data — Paystack tokenisation
  • No operations in unlicensed jurisdictions
  • No single-vendor dependency for payments

📉 Reduce

Lower likelihood or impact through controls.

  • Multi-region hosting failover (R6)
  • Fraud scoring & 3DS enforcement (R3)
  • Self-service KB to cap support creep (R10)
  • Referral & organic channel (R9)

✅ Accept

Retain the risk where cost of control exceeds benefit.

  • Wage inflation — market-driven, partially hedged with variable comp (R11)
  • Minor CAC drift within amber tolerance (R9)
35.9

Enterprise risk management — three lines of defence

Small team, clear separation: ownership, oversight, independent assurance.

First line

Own & manage

Founders, engineering and support own risk daily. They run the controls, log incidents, and raise KRIs that breach amber.

→ Founders · Support lead · Engineering lead
Second line

Set policy & monitor

Fractional DPO and external counsel set the risk framework, maintain the register, and challenge first-line decisions on compliance and FX.

→ Fractional DPO · External counsel · Finance lead
Third line

Independent assurance

External auditors, penetration testers and the NDPC audit verify that controls actually work. Findings feed straight back into the register.

→ External audit · Pen test vendor · NDPC
